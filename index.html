My thesis

The next enterprise interface isn't another dashboard. It is an agent that can make progress inside clear boundaries, with evidence.

01 / Agency

Useful autonomy

Agents should own outcomes, not merely generate responses. Every degree of autonomy must earn its place through measurable utility.

02 / Trust

Control by design

Identity, permissions, policy, human approval and auditability belong in the architecture, not in a checklist added before launch.

03 / Evidence

Production truth

A compelling demonstration is only a hypothesis. Traces, evaluations, incidents and business outcomes reveal whether the system works.
